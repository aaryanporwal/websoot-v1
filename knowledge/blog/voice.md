# Voice

Blog posts should match the site voice: **playful, warm, technical**. First person, witty without trying too hard, concrete over abstract. Real project names, real tools, real outcomes.

Avoid corporate copy ("empowering digital transformation"), generic SaaS tone, and AI-slop phrasing.
